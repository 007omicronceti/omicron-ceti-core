# Deep Space Intelligence Hub: Omicron Ceti

> [!abstract] Overview
> **Omicron Ceti (Mira)** is a prototypical symbiotic binary star system located 299 light-years from Earth. It serves as a central intelligence node intersecting astrophysics, ancient historical tracking, and high-velocity interstellar mechanics.

## 1. Source Intelligence & Telemetry
The core data on the system's physical architecture is derived from non-optical spectrum scanning:
*   **[[galex-uv-telemetry|NASA GALEX Observations (2007)]]**: Details the 13-light-year ultraviolet tail and bow shock.
*   **[[chandra-xray-data|Chandra X-Ray Observations (2005)]]**: Details the accretion disk and X-ray emissions of the Mira B white dwarf.

## 2. Historical OSINT & Archeoastronomy
Mira fundamentally broke the ancient assumption that the heavens were permanent and unchanging.
*   **[[star-of-bethlehem-hypothesis|The Star of Bethlehem Hypothesis]]**: Cross-references Mira's 332-day variable cycle with the 7-6 BC planetary conjunctions.
