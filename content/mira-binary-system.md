# Deep Space Intelligence Hub: Omicron Ceti (Mira)

> [!abstract] Dossier Overview
> **Omicron Ceti (Mira)** is a prototypical symbiotic binary star system located 299 light-years from Earth. Moving at supersonic speeds, it leaves a 13-light-year ultraviolet wake. Historically, its 332-day disappearing cycle shattered the Aristotelian concept of an unchanging universe and makes it the primary astronomical candidate for the biblical Star of Bethlehem.
