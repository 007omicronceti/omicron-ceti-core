# The Star of Bethlehem Hypothesis

The hypothesis that the [[mira-binary-system|Omicron Ceti]] binary is the mechanical engine behind the biblical "Star of Bethlehem" (Matthew 2:1-12) rests on two distinct astronomical alignments.

## 1. The Disappearing Act
The biblical text describes a star that appeared, vanished while the Magi traveled, and later reappeared to guide them. 
- Mira's 332-day variable cycle mechanically matches this. It fluctuates from a highly visible magnitude 2.0 (easily seen by the naked eye) down to magnitude 10 (completely invisible without modern optics).

## 2. The 7-6 BC Triple Conjunction
Ancient Babylonian and Persian astronomers (the Magi) were intensely focused on the constellation Pisces in 7-6 BC due to a rare triple conjunction of Jupiter and Saturn.
- **Line of Sight:** Mira is located just 30 degrees East of where this conjunction occurred. 
- **The Event:** Observers tracking the planetary convergence would have had their visual field perfectly aligned to witness Mira unexpectedly flare to maximum brightness, vanish for months, and reappear.
