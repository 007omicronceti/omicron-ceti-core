# Chandra X-Ray Observations (2005)

Observations by the Chandra X-Ray Observatory isolated the high-energy emissions coming from the companion star, Mira B, located 70 AU away from the primary red giant in the [[mira-binary-system]].

## The Accretion Engine
Mira B acts as a gravitational vacuum. As the primary star (Mira A) sheds its outer envelope into space at a rate of one Earth mass per decade, Mira B captures a fraction of this stellar wind. 
- The captured gas forms a rapidly spinning accretion disk around the white dwarf.
- The friction and magnetic interactions within this disk generate intense X-ray emissions.
