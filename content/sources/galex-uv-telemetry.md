# NASA GALEX Observations (2007)

In 2007, the Galaxy Evolution Explorer (GALEX) scanned the [[mira-binary-system|Omicron Ceti system]] in the ultraviolet spectrum, revealing a structure completely invisible to optical telescopes.

## Telemetry Data
- **Wake Length:** 13 light-years long.
- **Velocity:** 130 km/sec (291,000 mph).
- **Historical Record:** The tail contains 30,000 years of shed material (carbon, oxygen, and other heavy elements).
- **Mechanism:** As Mira A plows through the interstellar medium, a bow shock forms ahead of it, pushing the shed material backward into a glowing UV wake.
