# Omicron Ceti (`ο Ceti` / Mira) — Master Astrophysics & Primary-Source Archive

**Omicron Ceti (Mira)** is the archetype of all **Mira-type long-period pulsating variable stars**: a thermally pulsing Asymptotic Giant Branch (TP-AGB) red giant (~1.18 $M_\odot$, ~300–350 light-years away in **Cetus**) paired with an accreting white dwarf companion (**Mira B / VZ Ceti**) and plowing through the local interstellar medium at **~130 km/s**, leaving behind a **13-light-year far-ultraviolet bow-shock wake**.

This vault aggregates **verified primary telemetry** (NASA GALEX, Chandra, Hubble, ESO/ALMA, AAVSO), **peer-reviewed astrophysics**, and **multilingual pre-telescopic primary records** across East Asian, Middle Eastern, and European traditions.
